splat

A 3D Gaussian splat as a glasses-free 3D element, in one SDK call.

The page is ordinary flat HTML. The tile below is a <canvas> handed to addSplat(), which renders the splat with the PlayCanvas engine and renders it once per eye. If the .sog records the camera it was captured with, the tile opens at that camera and converges on the subject; otherwise it auto-frames the subject on the zero-disparity plane and turns it slowly. On the DisplayXR Browser it weaves to glasses-free 3D and looks around as you move; in any other browser you get the same asset, flat. Drag to tilt it (it relaxes back when you let go), scroll to zoom.

No default asset yet. Load one with ?url= (see Bring your own splat below).

What to look for

On hardware the subject should have real depth and shift perspective as you move your head, while the 2D plate over it stays perfectly crisp 2D. That plate is registered with handle.exclude() — the weave composites it rather than interlacing it.

Bring your own splat

Append ?url=… to load your own splat, same-origin or CORS-enabled: a .sog (preferred: Morton-ordered and roughly 15–20× smaller than the equivalent PLY), a .ply, or the lod-meta.json of a Streamed SOG, which streams only the chunks the view needs under a splat budget. Add &engine=spark to render the same tile with Spark (three.js) instead, the SDK's other backend, which also reads .spz, .splat and .ksplat but not a Streamed SOG.
