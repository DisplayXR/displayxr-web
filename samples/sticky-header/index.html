Sticky header over a woven wall

Scroll. The 3D tiles must pass under the translucent bar as flat 2D — never woven over it, never seaming into it. The bar is ordinary sticky HTML with no inline-3D wiring at all: auto-chrome finds it and excludes it from every tile's weave. This is the chrome-over-scrolling-tile occlusion path, in a page you can read.
