lift — Convert to 3D

Any <video>, <img> or <canvas>, lifted in place: live 3D while a video plays, an explorable 3D scene when it pauses (drag to orbit). Alt-click any media to lift it (resolveMediaAt). The conversion is the runtime's: in the DisplayXR Browser the lift is native (…); anywhere else lift() rejects (unavailable) unless a lift provider is given. Query: mode=auto|live|explore (default: the SDK's — live for a video), video=, image=, quality=, maxAngle=, explore={…} (explore view: comfort, pivotTargetM, eyes). Remote SHARP (demo only): lift=remote-sharp lifts pauses/stills on the SHARP worker (sharp= endpoint, default the same-origin proxy /api/sharp/predict of serve.py --sharp; auth=google [+ login=] for a hosted endpoint's popup sign-in).
