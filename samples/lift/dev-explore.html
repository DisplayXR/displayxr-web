Convert to 3D — explore renderer (dev harness)

Synthesises a two-layer image-aligned 3DGS PLY (a procedural photo + analytic depth: three boxes in front of a wall and floor), loads it through js/lift/explore.js, and renders it via createInline3D().addScene — or, off the DisplayXR Browser, through synthetic eye views (?views=sbs, default) or the flat path (?views=mono). Drag to orbit (±15°, relaxes on release). Query: grid=0.5 (fraction of full res), axes=opencv, fit=cover|contain, sync=1 (gl.finish per frame: GPU-bound timing), reveal=0, rep=N (N draws per frame: GPU throughput), dpr=2 + store=sbs (size the backing store as the woven layer would: double-width at device res), ply=<url>&meta=<url> (a real lift's PLY + meta JSON instead of the synthetic scene), aspect=16/9.
