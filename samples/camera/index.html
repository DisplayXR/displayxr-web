camera

The stereo camera as a primitive (@displayxr/inline3d/camera): open the best camera, show a correctly mirrored 3D self view, take a 3D photo, record a 3D clip. Files are plain side-by-side with the layout in the name (_2x1) and the stereo record inside the file, so they open in the player. On the DisplayXR Browser with a 3D display the self view is glasses-free 3D; anywhere else it is the left eye, flat — and the files are still real pairs when the camera is one.

URL params: ?camera=stereo prefer a pair · ?camera=mono · ?camera=synthetic a generated side-by-side pair with a known disparity (no camera needed; what the eye-swap check uses) · ?debug logs. A pair is detected by the DisplayXR Browser's displayxrStereo track settings or by a device delivering wider than 2.5:1 frames. Developer guide: docs/camera.md. Preview tier — sdk-stability.md, RFC 0003 §4. The call built on this is samples/call-embed/.
