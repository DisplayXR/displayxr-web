hello-cube

A glasses-free 3D element inside an ordinary web page — the DisplayXR inline-3D "hello world".

Everything on this page is flat 2D HTML except the canvas: it requests an inline-3d WebXR session, binds an XRDisplayLayer, and renders a rotating cube once per eye, re-projected off-axis from your eyes every frame. In the DisplayXR Browser on a DisplayXR display it weaves to glasses-free 3D and looks around as you move; in any other browser it is a plain spinning cube.

What to look for

The cube sits just behind the glass and turns; its nearest corners come gently out of it. The 2D text around it stays flat and crisp.

How it works

Plain WebGL into the canvas, one draw per eye with that eye's own view. Read Making 3D that looks right before you start your own, then copy a starter template.
