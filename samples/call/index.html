call

A 3D video call in one addCall(): start a call, share the invite link (or its QR code), and everyone with a 3D display sees the others in 3D — peer-to-peer, up to four people. A stereo camera (one wide side-by-side device) is picked up automatically and sent as a real stereo pair; any other webcam is sent as 2D. On any other browser the call is an ordinary 2D call.
